## 1.1.1 - 2026-10-01

**Fixed: Shen'dralas had no zone level range.** Pointing at it on the continent map now shows
Shen'dralas (40-45), the levels of the creatures that live there in the beta, as Wowhead's Forever
database records them. The client itself holds no levels for the zone, and no guide gave one when
1.1.0 was made, so it had been left out.

**Fixed: Mount Hyjal showed (60).** The zone guides send players there at 60, but its creatures
start at 55, so it now shows Mount Hyjal (55-60). The ranges of the other two Forever zones,
Zephras Isle (1-12) and Riverglades (35-45), were checked the same way and stand.

498 checks in normal, bare and no-enum modes alike.
